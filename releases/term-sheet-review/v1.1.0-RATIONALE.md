# Rationale for v1.1.0 Changes

This document explains why two new extraction fields were added in v1.1.0, with direct references to the client brief.

---

## Source: Tom Harrow's Requirements

In the discovery call, Tom Harrow (Ops & Knowledge Lead) explicitly listed **9 specific terms** that the term-sheet review skill must extract. The v1.0.0 skill covered 7 of these. Two were missing:

### 1. Founder Vesting Schedule

**Tom's exact words:**
> "Founder Vesting Schedule: The duration and the cliff (the standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff; we need to flag any deviation). Acceleration terms: Is there 'single-trigger' or 'double-trigger' acceleration of vesting upon a sale of the company?"

**Why it matters (from Tom):**
- Standard is 4 years total, 1-year cliff
- Single-trigger acceleration (founders fully vest on sale) misaligns incentives post-acquisition — should be flagged as 🔴 Review
- Double-trigger (vest on sale + termination) is market standard

**v1.0.0 gap:** This field was not in the Key Terms table or the market-standard baseline.

### 2. Legal Fees & Expenses

**Tom's exact words:**
> "Legal Fees & Expenses: Who pays the legal fees for the round, and is there a cap on the investor's legal fees that the startup has to cover?"

**Why it matters (from Tom):**
- Company typically pays investor's reasonable legal fees
- Caps are standard: £10k–£25k early stage, £25k–£50k Series A
- Uncapped or unusually high caps should be flagged

**v1.0.0 gap:** This field was not extracted at all.

---

## Tom's Success Criterion

> "If Claude can consistently pull these nine items out of any draft PDF, map them to a structured summary, and flag deviations from our standard positions, that is 90% of the battle won for our term-sheet review."

v1.0.0 covered 7/9 items (78%). v1.1.0 covers 9/9 items (100%).

---

## Changes Made

| File | Change |
|------|--------|
| `SKILL.md` | Added "Founder vesting schedule" and "Legal fees & expenses" to the Key Terms table (step 3) |
| `market-standard-terms.md` | Added baseline definitions for vesting (4yr/1yr cliff, double-trigger) and legal fees (capped amounts by stage) |
| `instrument-applicability.md` | Added rows for both fields — both are "Relevant" for priced rounds/seed, "Expected (data room)" for SAFEs/notes |
| `eval/eval-results.md` | Re-ran all 4 test cases; all now extract the new fields (marked "Not stated" where absent from source docs, per extraction discipline) |
| `CHANGELOG.md` | Added v1.1.0 entry |

---

## Validation

The sample term sheets (GreenGrid, Nimbus, Anchorline, Solace) do **not** contain founder vesting or legal fees information — this is realistic, as these terms often live in separate documents (Shareholders' Agreement, side letters). The skill correctly marks them as "Not stated" and flags their absence as ⚪ Omission where relevant.

This matches Tom's requirement: *"If a term is absent, mark it 'Not stated' — do not infer, estimate, or fill from 'typical' deals."*
