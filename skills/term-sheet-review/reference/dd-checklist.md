# Harrow & Vale LLP — Standard Due Diligence Checklist

*Priya Vale's fixed DD checklist — applied to every funding-round/M&A matter regardless of instrument type. Any skill built to speed up DD review must check documents against this exact list, not invent its own categories.*

## 1. Corporate Structure
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation
- [ ] Articles of Association (current, as amended)
- [ ] Register of members / cap table
- [ ] Register of directors and secretaries
- [ ] Group structure chart (if applicable)

## 2. Share Capital & Instruments
- [ ] Full capitalisation table, fully diluted
- [ ] All outstanding SAFEs, convertible instruments, and option grants
- [ ] Share option scheme rules and grant letters
- [ ] Any side letters affecting share rights

## 3. Material Contracts
- [ ] Customer contracts above £50,000 annual value
- [ ] Supplier/vendor agreements with exclusivity or minimum-spend terms
- [ ] Lease agreements
- [ ] Loan agreements and other debt instruments

## 4. Intellectual Property
- [ ] IP assignment agreements from founders and contractors
- [ ] Registered trademarks/patents (if any)
- [ ] Open-source licence usage summary

## 5. Employment
- [ ] Employment agreements for all employees
- [ ] Contractor/consultancy agreements
- [ ] Any employment tribunal claims or disputes

## 6. Litigation & Compliance
- [ ] Outstanding or threatened litigation
- [ ] Regulatory correspondence or investigations
- [ ] Data protection / GDPR compliance summary (including any DPIA)

## 7. Financials & Tax
- [ ] Last two years' audited (or management) accounts
- [ ] Tax filings and any HMRC correspondence
- [ ] R&D tax credit claims (if any)

## 8. Insurance
- [ ] Directors' & officers' liability insurance
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance (if applicable)

## 9. Related-Party Transactions
- [ ] Any transactions between the company and its directors/major shareholders
