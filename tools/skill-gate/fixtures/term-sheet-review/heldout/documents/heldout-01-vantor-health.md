## Heads of Terms — Proposed Simple Agreement for Future Equity

Further to our meetings in May, we are pleased to set out below the principal terms on which
Marchmont Ventures LLP ("we", "the Investor") would be prepared to invest in Vantor Health Ltd
("the Company"). These heads of terms are intended to summarise our discussions and to form
the basis of full documentation.

**1. The investment.** We would subscribe £1,200,000 for 15% of the fully diluted share
capital of the Company, implying a post-money valuation of £6,000,000. The subscription would
be made under a simple agreement for future equity.

**2. Conversion.** The investment would convert into the most senior class of shares issued in
the Company's next equity financing raising not less than £2,000,000 in aggregate. Conversion
would occur at a 22% discount to the price per share paid by investors in that financing,
subject to a valuation cap of £9,000,000 on a pre-money basis. Where both the discount and the
cap would apply, whichever produces the greater number of shares for the Investor shall apply.

**3. Return on the investment.** Interest would accrue on the subscription amount at 6% per
annum, compounding annually, and would convert alongside the principal. If conversion has not
occurred within 24 months of completion, the Investor may at its option require repayment of
the subscription amount together with accrued interest, or convert at the valuation cap.

**4. Preference on a sale.** On a sale of the Company or other liquidity event, the Investor
would be entitled to receive one and one half times its subscription amount in priority to all
other shareholders, and would thereafter participate with the ordinary shareholders in the
distribution of remaining proceeds on an as-converted basis.

**5. Protection against a lower price.** Should the Company issue shares at a price per share
below the price at which the Investor's investment converted, the Investor's holding would be
adjusted so that its effective price per share equals that lower price.

**6. Board.** The Board would comprise four directors: two appointed by the Investor, one
founder director, and one non-executive director appointed by the Investor with the consent of
the founders.

**7. Matters requiring Investor consent.** The following would require the prior written
consent of the Investor: any amendment to the Company's articles; the sale of the Company; the
creation of any class of shares ranking in priority to or equally with the Investor's shares;
the incurring of any indebtedness exceeding £100,000; approval of the Company's annual budget;
and the appointment of any employee or contractor at a total annual remuneration exceeding
£45,000.

**8. Founder commitments.** The founders' existing shareholdings would be made subject to
vesting over three years from completion, with a six month cliff. On a sale of the Company all
unvested founder shares would vest in full immediately.

**9. Costs.** The Company would bear its own costs and the legal and professional costs
incurred by the Investor in connection with the investment.

**10. Exclusivity.** For a period of 120 days from the date of your countersignature, the
Company would not solicit, entertain or continue discussions with any other prospective
investor in relation to any equity or quasi-equity investment.

**11. Conditions.** Completion would be conditional upon satisfactory completion of legal and
financial due diligence, and upon the Investor's investment committee granting final approval.

**12. Status of these terms.** Save for paragraphs 10 and 12, these heads of terms are not
intended to create legally binding obligations between the parties.

We should be glad to discuss any aspect of the above. We remain enthusiastic about the
Company's prospects and hope to move to documentation promptly.
