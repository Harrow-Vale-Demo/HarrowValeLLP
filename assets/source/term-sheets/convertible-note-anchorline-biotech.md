# ANCHORLINE BIOTECH LTD — CONVERTIBLE LOAN NOTE (Summary of Principal Terms)
*Mock document — Synthetic Signal hackathon exercise.*

**Issuer:** Anchorline Biotech Ltd · **Noteholder:** Fenwick Life Sciences Fund II LP · **Instrument:** Unsecured Convertible Loan Note · **Date:** 19 June 2026

- **Principal Amount:** £500,000
- **Interest Rate:** 8% per annum, accruing and compounding annually, payable on conversion/redemption/maturity (not cash-paid during term).
- **Maturity Date:** 24 months from issue. If not converted/repaid, Company repays Principal + accrued interest in cash.
- **Conversion Discount:** 15% to price per share in a Qualifying Financing.
- **Valuation Cap:** £8,000,000 (pre-money). Conversion price = lower of Discount Price or Valuation Cap price.
- **Qualifying Financing:** equity financing raising ≥£2,000,000.
- **Conversion Mechanics:** on Qualifying Financing, Principal + accrued interest auto-converts into the preferred class issued, at the Conversion Price.
- **Change of Control:** Noteholder elects (a) Principal + accrued interest + **1.5x premium**, or (b) conversion into Ordinary Shares at Valuation Cap price.
- **Events of Default:** non-payment at maturity, insolvency, material breach unremedied within 30 days.
- **Security:** None — unsecured, ranks behind secured creditors.
- **Governing Law:** England and Wales.

- **8% compounding interest** — on the higher side; flag.
- **1.5x change-of-control premium** — aggressive/non-standard; flag prominently.
- Pre-money valuation cap (founder-friendlier than post-money).
- Unsecured, ranks behind secured creditors — note for DD/risk.
- No pro-rata rights mentioned — flag as **missing**.
