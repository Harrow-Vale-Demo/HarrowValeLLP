# NIMBUS ROBOTICS LTD — SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)
*Mock document — Synthetic Signal hackathon exercise.*

**Company:** Nimbus Robotics Ltd (England and Wales)
**Investor:** Cordwainer Angels LLP
**Date:** 14 March 2026

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purchase Amount | £750,000 |
| Valuation Cap | £6,000,000 (post-money) |
| Discount Rate | 20% |
| Interest | None (SAFE — not a debt instrument) |
| Maturity Date | None |
| Most Favoured Nation (MFN) | Yes — Investor receives benefit of any more favourable terms granted to a subsequent SAFE investor prior to conversion |
| Pro Rata Rights | Yes, in the Company's next Equity Financing |
| Conversion | Automatically converts to Safe Preferred Stock upon an Equity Financing of at least £1,000,000, at the lower of (a) the Valuation Cap price or (b) the Discount Rate price |

### Events
- **Equity Financing.** Auto-issues Safe Preferred Stock: greater of Purchase Amount ÷ Discount Price, or Purchase Amount ÷ Safe Price.
- **Liquidity Event.** Investor's option: cash equal to Purchase Amount, or Common Stock = Purchase Amount ÷ Liquidity Price.
- **Dissolution Event.** Cash payment equal to Purchase Amount, immediately prior to / concurrent with dissolution.

### Definitions (excerpt)
- "Equity Financing" = bona fide capital-raising transaction issuing Preferred Stock at a fixed valuation.
- "Discount Price" = price per share of Standard Preferred Stock in the Equity Financing × Discount Rate.

### Company Representations
Duly incorporated, validly existing, good standing under laws of England and Wales.

*Governing law: England and Wales.*

- No maturity / no interest — standard for a SAFE.
- Pro rata rights present.
