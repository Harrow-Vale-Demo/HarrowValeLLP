# GreenGrid Analytics Ltd — Articles of Association (Extract)

**Company number:** 14829371
**Adopted:** 2 May 2026 (concurrent with Series A completion)

## 3. Share Capital

3.1 The Company's share capital is divided into Ordinary Shares and Series A Preferred Shares, each with a nominal value of £0.001.

3.2 The rights attaching to the Series A Preferred Shares are as set out in Article 7 (Liquidation Preference) and Article 9 (Protective Provisions).

## 7. Liquidation Preference

7.1 On a Liquidation Event, the holders of Series A Preferred Shares shall be entitled to receive, in priority to the holders of Ordinary Shares, an amount equal to the greater of:
(a) 1x the Series A Original Issue Price per share; or
(b) the amount such holder would have received had all Series A Preferred Shares converted to Ordinary Shares immediately prior to the Liquidation Event.

## 8. Board Composition

8.1 The Board shall consist of three Directors: two appointed by holders of a majority of the Ordinary Shares, and one appointed by holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred Shares.

## 9. Protective Provisions

9.1 For so long as at least 10% of the Series A Preferred Shares issued at completion remain outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred Shares:
(a) amend these Articles in a manner adverse to the Series A Preferred Shares;
(b) effect a sale of the Company;
(c) create or issue any class of shares ranking senior to or pari passu with the Series A Preferred Shares;
(d) incur indebtedness in excess of £250,000 outside the ordinary course of business.
