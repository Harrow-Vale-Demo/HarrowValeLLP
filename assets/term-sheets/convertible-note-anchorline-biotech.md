# ANCHORLINE BIOTECH LTD — CONVERTIBLE LOAN NOTE

## Summary of Principal Terms

**Issuer:** Anchorline Biotech Ltd
**Noteholder:** Fenwick Life Sciences Fund II LP
**Instrument:** Unsecured Convertible Loan Note
**Date:** 19 June 2026

---

**Principal Amount:** £500,000

**Interest Rate:** 8% per annum, accruing and compounding annually, payable on conversion, redemption, or maturity (not paid in cash during the term).

**Maturity Date:** 24 months from the date of issue. If not converted or repaid by the Maturity Date, the Company shall repay the Principal Amount plus accrued interest in cash.

**Conversion Discount:** 15% discount to the price per share paid by investors in a Qualifying Financing.

**Valuation Cap:** £8,000,000 (pre-money). On conversion, the conversion price shall be the lower of (a) the Discount Price and (b) the price implied by the Valuation Cap.

**Qualifying Financing:** Any equity financing raising gross proceeds of at least £2,000,000 in a single transaction or series of related transactions.

**Conversion Mechanics:** Upon a Qualifying Financing, the outstanding Principal Amount plus accrued interest shall automatically convert into the same class of preferred shares issued in that Qualifying Financing, at the Conversion Price.

**Change of Control:** If a Change of Control occurs prior to conversion or repayment, the Noteholder may elect to receive either (a) repayment of the Principal Amount plus accrued interest and a 1.5x premium, or (b) conversion into Ordinary Shares immediately prior to completion at the Valuation Cap price.

**Events of Default:** Non-payment at maturity, insolvency, or a material breach of the note instrument unremedied within 30 days of notice.

**Security:** None — this is an unsecured instrument, ranking behind any secured creditors of the Company.

**Governing Law:** England and Wales.
